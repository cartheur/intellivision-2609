# Mattel Aquarius Thermal Printer — Replacement Paper

The Mattel Aquarius thermal printer uses **4 3/8 in. (111–112 mm) wide thermal paper**.  Modern POS/receipt-printer thermal rolls in this width are suitable replacements.

## Recommended starting point

Try a **4 3/8 in. x 80 ft** thermal roll first.  Its approximately 2 in. outer diameter makes it the safest readily available size to test in the vintage printer.

- [Freedom Electronics: 4 3/8 in. x 80 ft](https://freedomelectronics.com/product/1220-4-3-8-thermal-paper-80) — sold individually.
- [ThermalRoll: 4 3/8 in. x 80 ft (50-roll case)](https://www.thermalroll.com/4-3-8-x-80-thermal-paper-50-rolls/) — 2 in. roll diameter; 7/16 in. core; coated side out.

## Other current sizes

- [Prinko: 4 3/8 in. x 328 ft](https://prinko.com/products/4-3-8-x-328-thermal-printer-paper-rolls) — about 3 3/8 in. diameter; buy only if the printer's roll compartment has been measured to accommodate it.

## Fit and loading checks

Paper width alone is not enough.  Before ordering in bulk, measure the original roll or the printer's paper compartment for its maximum **outer roll diameter** and **core/spindle opening**.  Also ensure the thermal coating faces the printhead; a roll installed backwards will feed but will not print.

Contemporary Aquarius coverage specifies “special thermal paper 4-3/8 inches wide”: [Compute!, June 1983](https://www.atarimagazines.com/compute/issue37/low_cost_printers.php).  The Aquarius Printer is an Alphacom 42-family thermal printer, which helps explain the continued availability of this paper width.
